Sanctuary Pals Privacy Policy

Cozy Daily Wellness & Companion App by Jitabrata Pati

Last Updated: November 2026 • v1.0.0

1. Local Storage on Your Device

Sanctuary Pals stores your daily wellness activity directly on your device using local storage (AsyncStorage). This includes your hydration logs, step counts, focus timer sessions, mood check-ins, breathing completions, journal entries, and Sanctuary decor progress.

2. Optional Google Sign-In & Cloud Firestore Backup

You can use Sanctuary Pals in Guest Mode without creating an account. If you choose to connect Google Sign-In, Sanctuary Pals uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore to securely store your Name, Email address, and Sanctuary progress under your unique Firebase UID strictly for cross-device backup and synchronization.

3. No Third-Party Ad Tracking or Data Selling

We respect your privacy and peace of mind. Sanctuary Pals does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not use third-party advertising trackers or behavioral ad networks.

4. Account & Data Deletion Instructions

You can permanently delete your Firebase account, Cloud Firestore backup, and all local app data at any time directly inside the app:

Open Sanctuary Pals → Settings → Delete Account & All Data

Alternatively, you may request permanent deletion of your account and associated cloud data by emailing jitabrata.dev@gmail.com from your registered email address.
